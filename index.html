Privacy Policy for Unbolt Pals

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Unbolt Pals: Screw Puzzle ("the app", "the game") handles information. The app is published by the developer of Unbolt Pals ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • The app does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use some device data as described below.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ourselves collect, store or receive any personal information. We run no servers that receive your data, and we do not sell personal information.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, coins, boosters, settings and language choice in local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (the Android advertising identifier) and device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language);
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data (ads shown, clicked, crashes and performance).

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy settings in the app when it is shown.
  • Android settings: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.
  • Ad content: we limit ads to a family-friendly content rating.

5. Permissions and reminders

The app asks for only what it needs:

  • Internet and Network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: so Google AdMob can serve and measure ads (see section 4).
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer): to show optional daily reminders (for example "Your Daily Challenge is ready"). The reminders are created on your device; no information about them is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings → Reminders or in your Android notification settings, and the app asks before it first sends any.
  • Receive boot completed and Wake lock: only so a reminder you already allowed can still be delivered after the phone restarts.

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files, calendar or precise location.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share personal information. Ad data is processed by Google as described above. Data in transit to ad services uses HTTPS.

8. Data deletion and your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove all locally stored game data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over data processed by Google; use your Google account's data and privacy controls.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
